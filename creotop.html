The Four-Season Reality

Canvas biminis and generic aftermarket caps fail the Pacific Northwest test.

Traditional Canvas Enclosures

  • Deteriorates under UV, driving rain, and winter snow loads
  • Restricted helm visibility through clouded or scratched vinyl
  • Continual maintenance, zipper failures, and replacement every 4–6 years
  • No structural capacity for radar, solar panels, or heavy marine arrays

The CreoTop™ Standard

  • Permanent, vacuum-consolidated composite sandwich construction
  • Integrated recessed tracks for crystal-clear rigid polycarbonate panels
  • Molded-in wire chases keeping electrical runs 100% dry and hidden
  • Internally reinforced hard points engineered for radar domes, antennas, and high-wattage solar arrays

Engineered Architecture

Every CreoTop is built to exacting naval laminate schedules.

01

Closed-Cell Foam Core

High-density structural core delivers extraordinary shear strength and rigid thermal insulation while keeping weight aloft to an absolute minimum to protect vessel stability.

02

Internal Wireways

Dedicated conduits cast internally through the structural matrix prior to lamination. Pull radar, NMEA 2000 feeds, spreader lights, and solar wiring cleanly without exposed exterior conduit.

03

Perimeter Drip Rails & Valances

Engineered rain gutters divert driving coastal spray away from helm seating. Precision downward valances provide factory mounting for 3-season or 4-season enclosures.

04

Laminated Hard Points

High-density composite and aluminum backing plates embedded at key structural load zones for radar pedestals, searchlights, and heavy-duty 316 stainless stanchions.

05

Custom Vessel Geometry

Each mold is tailored to your yacht’s camber, rake, and beam—preserving the authentic visual aesthetic of classic coastal hulls rather than looking like an afterthought.

06

Isophthalic Marine Finish

Coated in premium marine gelcoat or sprayed in commercial two-part polyurethane, precisely matched to your deck and hull tones for decades of maintenance-free service.

The CreoTop™ Execution Flow

STEP 01

Deck Geometry Capture

We map your bridge or aft deck layout, rake angles, and mounting stations to ensure seamless mechanical fitment and proper weight balance.

STEP 02

Matrix & Core Formulation

Structural foam cores are shaped, internal wire runs routed, and reinforcement plates embedded for your specific antenna and solar loads.

STEP 03

Lamination & Consolidation

Multi-axial fiberglass matrices are consolidated with marine-grade resins to guarantee absolute structural bonding and zero water intrusion.

STEP 04

Hardware & Stanchions

Custom 316 stainless steel supports and deck brackets are fabricated and aligned to anchor the structure firmly against coastal seas.
